# LH-007 — Core Challenge Pack and Match Presentation

Day: 2 AM
Depends on: LH-006
Commit: feat(gameplay): ship core challenge engine pack

## Product outcome

Players experience a varied, readable, server-driven match rather than repeated wait-for-state rounds. Hearts, energy, outcomes, elimination, spectating, and winner presentation remain coherent across every engine.

## Launch engines owned

1. Wait for state/color — migrate and visually complete the existing engine.
2. No-touch trap.
3. Reverse swipe.
4. Precision tap.
5. Memory sequence.
6. Multitouch hold.

These engines are selected first because they create visible variety without making sensor, audio, or pair reliability the Day 2 blocker.

## Shared challenge experience

Every engine uses the same product rhythm:

1. Short intro with round/category/hearts.
2. Preloaded definition and future start.
3. Authoritative active window.
4. One accepted attempt or declared continuous input.
5. Reasoned result.
6. Hearts, energy, and standing update.
7. Short automatic transition.

The shared HUD always makes hearts, energy, challenge number/category, and timer legible. No engine invents a separate navigation system or recomputes authoritative results.

## Engine behavior

### Wait for state

- Early tap fails.
- Tap after cyan/blue activation and through the inclusive deadline succeeds.
- Shape, motion, and haptic support accompany color.

### No-touch trap

- Touch inside the declared prohibited gameplay zone fails.
- Reaching the deadline untouched succeeds.
- System gestures and accessibility navigation are not gameplay touches.

### Reverse swipe

- Direction, distance, and time are definition-driven.
- The required swipe is opposite the displayed direction.
- A short touch is not a completed swipe.

### Precision tap

- The requested target and attempt count are explicit.
- Targets remain physically tappable across supported devices.
- Layout randomization cannot create unfair overlap or safe-area clipping.

### Memory sequence

- Preview and answer share one challenge identity and deadline plan.
- The answer screen cannot leak the answer through order, position, or animation.
- Reconnect restores the correct phase without replaying the preview improperly.

### Multitouch hold

- Two distinct contacts are held for the configured duration.
- Progress is visible.
- Lift/reset/failure behavior follows the published definition.
- Unsupported accessibility/input environments receive an eligible alternative.

## Hearts and result lifecycle

- Failure, prohibited action, or timeout removes at most one heart for the challenge.
- Duplicate submissions cannot duplicate loss or energy.
- Zero hearts immediately moves the player to spectator.
- Spectators see current state but cannot answer, use powers, target, or rejoin competitively.
- Results explain success, failure reason, timeout, invalid action, heart change, energy change, and elimination.
- If only one active player remains, show the immutable winner summary.
- Simultaneous final elimination follows the locked best-performer rule.

## Training

Every eligible engine becomes available in Training with:

- Instruction/demo.
- Practice input.
- Precise result reason.
- Fast retry.
- No competitive progression or match analytics.

## Accessibility at implementation time

- No color-only instruction/result.
- Reduced-motion-safe presentation.
- Screen-reader support for setup and result states.
- Text scaling bounds verified.
- Touch targets fair across phone sizes.
- Alternative mapping for an unsupported engine/capability.

## Out of scope

- Shake, tilt, audio, pair sync, and risk choice.
- Power use; energy display and earning remain visible.
- One-off downloadable scripts or hardcoded variants.

## Acceptance criteria

- A published catalog can deal a mixed six-engine match.
- Each engine supports success, failure, timeout, duplicate, exact-deadline, late, reconnect, and spectator cases where applicable.
- Training works for all eligible engines.
- Full three-heart to spectator to winner journey works.
- Results never disagree with the authoritative state.
- Reference screens 08 through 17, 20, 22, 23, 29, and 30 are represented where relevant.
- At least one unit path and one end-to-end simulation path exists per engine.
- A repeated multi-round simulation shows cooldown and no impossible catalog selection.
- Flutter, backend, contracts, admin regressions, builds, and simulations pass.

## Required evidence

- Visual evidence for intro, every owned engine, success, loss, elimination, spectator, and winner.
- Simulation matrix per engine and timing boundary.
- Reconnect during memory preview/answer and multitouch hold.
- Commit hash and clean tree.
