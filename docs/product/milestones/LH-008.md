# LH-008 — Complete Energy and Tactical Powers Loop

Day: 2 PM
Depends on: LH-005, LH-006, LH-007
Commit: feat(gameplay): complete energy and tactical powers

## Product outcome

Success changes strategy. Players fill four energy segments, receive a power exactly once, attack or defend in controlled between-round windows, and return to the challenge loop with one authoritative result.

## Energy lifecycle

- Standard success grants one energy unless an approved definition grants zero to three.
- Failure grants none.
- Meter contains four segments.
- Reaching four grants exactly one eligible power. The meter stays full until that power is consumed, then resets to zero.
- The grant uses the match-pinned power release and server-controlled weights.
- Inventory holds three total charges and one charge of each power.
- Full inventory produces a clear outcome; no reward disappears silently.
- Duplicate result events or reconnect cannot grant twice.

## Danger Pulse

- Offensive power used only in the between-round action window.
- Owner sees only eligible active targets.
- Cannot target self, spectator, protected player, or the same target on consecutive attacks.
- Target receives one clear server-controlled defense moment.
- Failed defense removes one heart, including the last heart.
- The target receives protection through the next standard challenge.
- Target selection expires safely when no eligible target remains.

## Shield

- Offered only when an incoming attack can affect the player.
- Consuming it cancels the attack heart loss.
- It cannot reverse a resolved loss.
- Duplicate use cannot consume a second charge.

## Reflection

- Returns an incoming Danger Pulse to the original sender.
- Reflection occurs once per attack chain.
- The reflected attack cannot be reflected again.
- The sender receives the normal defense opportunity and may shield.

## UX states

- Power unlocked.
- Inventory full.
- Offensive action available.
- Target selection.
- No eligible target.
- Incoming danger.
- Defense countdown.
- Shield used.
- Attack reflected.
- Attack succeeded/failed/expired.
- Protection active.
- Reconnected during every unresolved state.

No power control appears over or distracts from an active standard challenge.

## Configuration and governance

The match-pinned power release controls:

- Localized name/explanation.
- Mode availability and grant weight.
- Energy threshold relationship.
- Inventory limit.
- Use and defense windows.
- Target eligibility/protection.
- Reflection-chain rule.
- UI/audio/haptic assets.
- Pause, schedule, rollback, and audit.

Ordinary configuration cannot bypass the no-pay-to-win or core fairness constraints.

## Analytics events

Record real, privacy-conscious events for:

- Energy earned.
- Power granted and inventory-full outcome.
- Power offered, used, expired, and rejected.
- Target selection.
- Defense, shield, reflection, and resulting heart change.
- Concentrated targeting protection.

## Out of scope

- Paid or progression-purchased powers.
- More than the three launch powers.
- Power use by spectators.
- Arbitrary live mutation of inventory or hearts.

## Acceptance criteria

- Four valid successes grant exactly one power once.
- Inventory constraints are enforced and explained.
- Attack, normal defense, shield, and reflection each complete end to end.
- Reflection cannot loop.
- Illegal targets are rejected on both client and server.
- Two attackers racing for a protected target resolve deterministically and do not double-charge or double-damage.
- Disconnect/reconnect during grant, targeting, defense, shield, and reflection restores the same state and deadline.
- Zero-heart target becomes spectator exactly once.
- The next standard challenge begins once after power resolution.
- Screens 24 through 28 match the reference states.
- Power simulations, gameplay regression, mobile/admin tests, and builds pass.

## Required evidence

- One complete energy-to-power-to-resolution recording for each power.
- Illegal-target and reflection-loop rejection.
- Reconnect matrix across power phases.
- Audit/release record for a power configuration change affecting only new matches.
- Commit hash and clean tree.
