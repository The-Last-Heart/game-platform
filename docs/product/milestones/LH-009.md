# LH-009 — Advanced Challenge Pack

Day: 3 AM
Depends on: LH-007, LH-008
Commit: feat(gameplay): ship advanced challenge engine pack

## Product outcome

The launch catalog gains motion, balance, audio, social synchronization, and tactical risk without compromising fairness, accessibility, reconnection, or device compatibility.

## Launch engines owned

1. Shake.
2. Tilt balance.
3. Audio cue.
4. Pair synchronization.
5. Risk choice.

Voice recognition remains excluded. Incoming-danger defense is part of the powers loop and may reuse appropriate engine capabilities.

## Shake

- Required deliberate shake count is definition-driven.
- Show progress such as two of three.
- Filter sensor noise and reject impossible/unsafe values.
- Do not encourage violent movement.
- Devices/accessibility profiles without motion eligibility receive a published alternative.

## Tilt balance

- Move a visible object into a target and hold for the required time.
- Calibrate normal variance without creating a hidden advantage.
- Progress and target state are clear.
- Safe limits and alternative challenge are mandatory.

## Audio cue

- React after the declared audible pulse count.
- This challenge does not capture or recognize speech.
- Muted devices and audio-ineligible players receive synchronized visual/haptic equivalence.
- The alternative must preserve the same information and fair active window.

## Pair synchronization

- Pair two eligible active players.
- Both see partner readiness and joint result.
- Compare adjusted event timing using the match authority and the initial 500 ms tolerance.
- Network arrival order alone cannot decide success.
- Disconnect, elimination, or missing compatible partner triggers the published fair fallback.
- One player cannot submit on behalf of the other.

## Risk choice

- Present one clear choice between safe potential +1 energy and harder potential +3 energy.
- Choice deadline is authoritative.
- The selected follow-up definition and reward are pinned together.
- Reward is earned only after successful completion of that follow-up.
- Reconnect cannot reroll the choice, challenge, or reward.

## Catalog safeguards

- Declare device capability and accessibility eligibility.
- Cap sensor/audio/pair frequency per match.
- Prevent impossible pair assignment.
- Respect app version and asset availability.
- Preserve family/template repetition cooldown.
- Provide a complete non-sensor/non-audio fallback catalog.

## Training

- Shake, tilt, and audio alternatives can be practiced.
- Risk choice shows both consequence paths without granting competitive rewards.
- Pair sync uses an explicitly labeled practice partner or is omitted when a fair practice partner is unavailable.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every engine is fully definition-driven.
- Every engine supports exact-deadline, timeout, duplicate, late, background/resume, and reconnect behavior where applicable.
- A player with a no-motion/no-audio profile can complete a full match.
- Pair success/failure around 500 ms is deterministic and latency-tolerant.
- Partner disconnect never creates an impossible unavoidable heart loss.
- Risk reward cannot be claimed before the follow-up success or duplicated.
- A mixed launch catalog can produce repeated 12-round plus sudden-death matches without incompatible selection.
- Reference screens 12, 13, 18, 19, and 21 are faithfully represented.
- Unit, integration, simulator, mobile, backend, contracts, and build checks pass.

## Required evidence

- Real-device or emulator capture for shake, tilt, and audio.
- Two-player pair-sync boundary evidence.
- Capability fallback full-match evidence.
- Risk choice success/failure/reconnect matrix.
- Commit hash and clean tree.
