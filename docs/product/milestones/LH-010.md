## In scope

- Energy charge on success; unlock thresholds (server-configured)
- Powers: danger pulse (attack), shield, reflect
- Target selection + resolve attack/defense server-authoritatively
- Return to challenge pool after power resolution
- Enforce **no pay-to-win** power purchases (pack rule 10)

## Out of scope

- Store / IAP
- Additional power types beyond the three base powers
- Inventing power balance without PO

## Open questions (PO)

1. Exact energy required to unlock each power
2. Whether multiple powers can be held / queueing rules
3. Whether spectators can observe power UX only (expected yes)

## Exit criteria

- Demo: survive → unlock → attack → shield or reflect → next challenge
- Contracts + simulations cover power races and illegal self-targets
- Visual review vs screens 24–28
