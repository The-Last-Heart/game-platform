# LH-010 — Full Challenge Studio and Content Operations

Day: 3 PM
Depends on: LH-006 through LH-009
Commit: feat(admin): deliver full challenge studio

## Product outcome

Authorized product/content teams can create, validate, preview, playtest, review, publish, schedule, pause, roll back, compose, translate, and analyze launch challenge content without editing code or redeploying the mobile app.

## Studio home

Show real linked summaries for:

- Drafts.
- Review queue.
- Scheduled releases.
- Published catalog.
- Paused challenges.
- Capability/accessibility coverage.
- Recent publish activity.
- Statistically unusual failure, timeout, disconnect, or abandonment.

Empty state replaces fake data.

## Challenge library

Provide working search, filters, safe sorting, pagination, and permission-aware actions for:

- ID/internal title.
- Family/category/difficulty.
- Lifecycle state.
- Supported locales.
- Capabilities and accessibility fallback.
- Eligible modes/rounds.
- Published version.
- Editors/reviewers/timestamps.
- Selection weight.
- Real performance indicators.

Safe bulk actions require explicit selection, consequence summary, confirmation, and audit.

## Guided editor

Use a structured product form for:

1. Identity and ownership.
2. Arabic instruction, hint, and outcome reasons.
3. Family/category/difficulty.
4. Timing.
5. Family parameters.
6. Input and success/failure behavior.
7. Heart and energy effects.
8. Assets, sounds, haptics, colors, and shapes.
9. Capability/accessibility and fallback.
10. Mode/round/weight/cooldown.
11. Minimum app version.
12. Review notes and change summary.

Validation uses understandable product language and prevents incomplete Arabic, unsafe timing/rewards, impossible touch/sensor values, unavailable assets, missing fallbacks, unsupported parameters, and non-viable catalogs.

Advanced structured inspection/export may exist, but arbitrary executable code is forbidden.

## Preview and playtest

- Live portrait phone preview in RTL by default.
- Intro, active, success, failure, timeout, and unsupported-capability states.
- Interactive playtest against the real reusable engine contract.
- Slow network, exact/late/duplicate submit, background, and reconnect scenarios.
- Six-seat synchronized timeline.
- Two-player pair view.
- Danger Pulse defense view.
- Clear test-data label and complete separation from production analytics.

## Review and releases

- Draft, submitted, in review, approved, scheduled, published, paused, and retired.
- Candidate freeze and meaningful diff.
- Reviewer, time, notes, validation results, and separation-of-duty enforcement.
- Human-readable release changelog.
- Immediate/scheduled publish.
- New-release rollback.
- Emergency pause/resume.
- Immutable active-match pins.
- Audit every sensitive transition.

## Catalog composer

Working controls for:

- Modes and eligible content.
- Round difficulty curve.
- Weights and cooldowns.
- Sensor/audio/pair/risk caps.
- Accessibility coverage.
- App-version support.
- Temporary exclusions and kill switches.
- Pre-publish proof that every supported profile can receive a fair full match.

## Localization center

- Versioned mobile and challenge catalogs.
- Arabic completeness mandatory.
- Missing/stale keys and fallback impact.
- Translator/reviewer workflow.
- RTL/LTR preview.
- Published catalog caching and safe fallback.
- Admin UI dictionaries remain separate.
- No per-string network request inside a challenge.

## Power, mode, reward control

Provide governed versioned surfaces for:

- Power names, copy, weights, eligibility, timing, protection, inventory, assets, scheduling, pause, and rollback.
- Six-player/three-heart/12-round/three-to-eight-second/four-energy defaults through reviewed releases, not casual toggles.
- Daily goals based on real supported events, with copy, eligibility, target, reward, schedule, repeat rules, and player-state preview.

## Challenge analytics

Per challenge/version:

- Attempts and matches.
- Success/failure/timeout/invalid-action.
- Heart loss and energy awarded.
- Meaningful completion-time percentiles.
- Early/prohibited interaction.
- Disconnect/background/fallback.
- Filters for app version, device family, locale, mode, difficulty, round, and release under privacy thresholds.

## Permissions

Use existing roles and granular permissions:

- content_manager manages permitted content and review.
- translator manages localization but not unrelated gameplay publishing.
- live_ops can pause/resume approved operational content but cannot edit draft content without permission.
- viewer is read-only.
- super_admin governs access and high-risk releases.

## Acceptance criteria

- An operator completes create to published new-match use without engineering intervention.
- All eleven families have structured family-specific forms.
- Preview uses the same contracts/parameters as mobile engines.
- Publish is impossible with missing Arabic or coverage holes.
- Scheduled publish, pause, resume, rollback, and retire work and are audited.
- Active matches remain pinned.
- Translation change reaches the next match through a versioned catalog and safe cache.
- Challenge analytics are based on actual attempts and show honest empty states.
- EN/LTR and AR/RTL work for every Studio route, table, form, dialog, toast, error, chart, and responsive layout.
- Permission enforcement exists on server and UI.
- Admin tests, real-browser flows, backend/contracts/mobile regressions, migrations, and builds pass.

## Required evidence

- Full catalog lifecycle recording.
- One preview/playtest per family.
- EN/LTR and AR/RTL desktop/mobile browser matrix.
- Permission matrix.
- Active-match pin and rollback proof.
- Commit hash and clean tree.
