## Product outcome

A first-time Arabic player can open the app, understand the game, choose a real mode, enter a real match, complete the existing playable loop, and rematch without encountering a placeholder, dead button, or fabricated status.

## Player journeys

1. First launch to Quick Survival to completed match to rematch.
2. Returning launch directly to home.
3. Create Private Room, share code, join six seats, ready once, and begin.
4. Join with invalid/full/expired code and receive an understandable recovery path.
5. Enter Training, practice wait-for-state, receive precise feedback, and retry.
6. Cancel public matchmaking without leaving a ghost seat.
7. Disconnect/reconnect during queue or lobby without duplicating the player.

## In scope

### Entry and identity

- Real splash with no fake delay.
- Three-step Arabic onboarding: hearts, timed action, energy/powers.
- Persist onboarding completion and provide a help route to replay it.
- Lightweight player identity using display name and the approved abstract color/avatar system.
- Validation, duplicate-name presentation inside a match, edit flow, and honest local/recovered identity state.

### Home and modes

- Reference-faithful home, mode selection, matchmaking, private lobby, ready, final summary, and rematch transitions.
- Home shows real level/points/activity/daily-goal data or omits the area.
- One dominant Play Now action.
- Quick Survival, Private Room, and Training are the only launch modes.
- Remove or disable from the rendered product any store, battle pass, tournament, team, chat, or future navigation.

### Quick Survival

- Real enqueue, seat progress, accepted state, timeout, cancel, and error recovery.
- Six accepted seats before start.
- Configurable bot-fill threshold controlled by Live Operations; bots are visibly and analytically distinguishable.
- Cancellation and expired reservation free capacity.

### Private Room

- Create and join by short room code.
- Native code sharing and copy confirmation.
- Six seats with host, ready, disconnected, empty, and bot states where relevant.
- Host cannot trigger duplicate countdowns.
- Leaving releases the seat.
- Resuming restores the same seat instead of creating another player.

### Training and rematch

- Training initially uses the existing wait-for-state engine and expands automatically as eligible engines ship.
- Training explains the input, displays reasoned result, and supports immediate retry.
- Training does not change competitive progression or analytics.
- Rematch creates a new match identity while preserving the party when possible.

### Experience states

- Loading, empty, offline, reconnecting, full room, invalid code, queue cancelled, queue timeout, and service unavailable.
- Arabic copy is concise and true RTL.
- No bottom navigation inside a match.

## Product rules

- Public and private competitive matches contain six accepted seats.
- Countdown starts once with one stable deadline.
- A stale socket cannot remove a resumed player.
- No screen may pretend that a queue, activity count, level, or reward exists when the backend has no such state.
- Mobile navigation must not expose incomplete future areas.

## Out of scope

- New challenge engines beyond wait-for-state.
- Full Challenge Studio.
- Full power interaction.
- Consumer email/password/social authentication.
- Store, currency spending, ranked leagues, friends, or chat.

## Acceptance criteria

- Cold start to public match to rematch succeeds end to end.
- Six private players can join once each and begin one countdown.
- Queue cancel leaves no reserved or active player.
- Invalid/full room errors have working alternative actions.
- Reconnect during lobby keeps identity, seat, host/ready state, and countdown.
- Training has real engine behavior and no competitive rewards.
- Every visible action changes real state.
- Screens 01 through 07, 29, and 30 match their intended reference states where applicable.
- Existing playable simulation, mobile tests, backend tests, admin regression, builds, and formatting remain green.

## Required evidence

- Screen captures for cold start, modes, queue, private lobby, ready, and rematch in Arabic.
- One observed six-seat public or bot-filled flow.
- One observed six-seat private-room flow.
- One lobby reconnect trace proving no duplicate seat/countdown.
- Commands and exact observed results.
- Commit hash and empty final git status.
