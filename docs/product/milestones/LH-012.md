# LH-012 — Product Intelligence, Fairness, Security, and Resilience

Day: 4 PM

## Product outcome

The full product remains trustworthy under timing boundaries, retries, reconnects, concurrency, invalid clients, service interruption, and operational pressure. Product decisions can be based on real, correctly defined measurements.

## Product analytics

Instrument and define:

- Onboarding completion.
- Home-to-queue conversion.
- Queue cancellation, timeout, and median/percentile wait.
- Queue-to-match conversion.
- Lobby completion.
- Full-match completion and duration.
- Challenge attempts, success, failure, timeout, invalid action, and repetition.
- Meaningful completion-time percentiles.
- Heart lifecycle and elimination.
- Energy and power grant/use/expiry.
- Attack/defense/shield/reflection and targeting concentration.
- Reconnect attempts and successful recovery.
- Spectator continuation.
- Sudden-death entry.
- Rematch selection.
- Error-free sessions.

Humans, bots, training, and competitive play are always distinguishable. Definitions, timezones, privacy thresholds, and retention are documented. No client-only event may award gameplay state.

## Timing and fairness hardening

- One authoritative future start and deadline per phase.
- Inclusive deadline across all engines.
- Monotonic client representation.
- Capped measured latency tolerance.
- One effective submission per player/challenge.
- Duplicate/retry returns same result.
- Ranking uses declared performance measure and fairness buckets where needed.
- Same round/release identity for all players.
- Late state jumps to current authority rather than replaying stale animation.
- Deterministic tie and simultaneous-elimination behavior.
- Pair timing uses adjusted event time and 500 ms rule.

## Reconnect and ownership matrix

Verify resume in:

- Queue.
- Private lobby.
- Ready countdown.
- Challenge intro.
- Every active engine.
- Result.
- Energy grant.
- Target selection.
- Incoming attack.
- Shield/reflection.
- Elimination/spectator.
- Winner summary/rematch.

Restore identity, seat, hearts, energy, inventory, phase, accepted attempt, restrictions, release pins, and deadlines. Newer connection owns the seat. Stale disconnect cannot remove it. Expired disconnected challenges follow normal timeout; no retry.

## Concurrency and recovery

- Sixth-seat joins and resume races.
- Countdown starts once.
- Two target attacks and protection races.
- Duplicate power use.
- Publish/rollback races.
- Last-super-admin changes.
- Idempotent login/logout/session revoke.
- Service restart with durable match/release history as designed.
- Redis/database outage behavior is explicit and recoverable.
- Graceful shutdown leaves no corrupt ownership or open handle.

## Security and abuse

- Validate every client input and state transition.
- Enforce permissions on server.
- Rate-limit login, queue, room code, realtime actions, admin search/export, and destructive operations appropriately.
- CSRF/origin/session protections remain intact.
- No resume/session/admin credential in logs, responses, audit, analytics, or archives.
- Reject unsupported app versions/content definitions safely.
- Do not trust device timestamps or client success claims.
- Define suspension behavior and prevent bypass through duplicate identity/session.
- Audit high-risk behavior without exposing sensitive payloads.

## Operational resilience

- Health/readiness distinguishes dependencies.
- Clear degraded, maintenance, unavailable, and recovery behavior.
- Migrations are ordered and idempotent.
- Backup and restore procedure is documented and rehearsed where the environment permits.
- Release rollback does not mutate historical matches.
- Kill switches work under load and show their effective state.
- Alerts/incident indicators use real conditions, not fake data.

## Performance targets for RC

Capture measured local/staging results instead of asserting unsupported scale:

- Countdown/challenge start skew across six clients.
- Realtime round-trip and resolution percentiles.
- Queue and lobby state convergence.
- Admin dashboard/query response under realistic seeded volume.
- Memory/CPU stability through repeated full matches.
- No leaked handles after test suites and graceful shutdown.

If production concurrency is not supplied, report measured capacity and the unverified boundary; do not invent a scale claim.

## Acceptance criteria

- Boundary matrix covers early, valid, exact, late, duplicate, retry, disconnect, resume, and stale socket.
- Repeated six-client full-match simulations converge with no contradictory state.
- All race tests have deterministic accepted outcomes.
- Analytics reconcile to authoritative event/match data.
- Security tests prove rejected origins, CSRF failures, invalid sessions, unauthorized roles, malformed realtime input, and secret redaction.
- Database/Redis interruption has observed product behavior and recovery evidence.
- Migration/bootstrap repeated execution is safe.
- Test processes exit without forceExit or open-handle warning.
- Dependency audit and release builds pass.
