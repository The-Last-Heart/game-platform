Privacy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This Privacy notice explains how The Last Heart (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile game, related websites (including tlh.wdigitallabs.com), and online services.

What we collect

  • Account & identity data needed to play online (for example display name and player identifiers).
  • Gameplay & match data such as challenge results, scores, and connection events required to run authoritative matches.
  • Device & technical data such as app version, approximate network metadata, and diagnostics needed for reliability and abuse prevention.
  • Admin console data when you are an authorized operator (login sessions, audit actions).

How we use information

  • To operate matches, presence, leaderboards, and content delivery.
  • To secure accounts, prevent fraud, and keep the service stable.
  • To improve challenges, performance, and player experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations when required.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with infrastructure providers that host our servers and networks (for example cloud hosting and CDN/DNS), solely to run the service, under appropriate safeguards.

Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including match integrity, security, and legal requirements. You may request deletion of account-linked data where applicable law allows.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your choices

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us using the details below to exercise those rights.
